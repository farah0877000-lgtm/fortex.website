About FORTEX

One Brand. Every Layer of Protection.

FORTEX is a specialist manufacture of personal protective equipment built for the realities of industrial work. From face and eye protection to hand safety and protective workwear, our catalogue is designed to cover the professional from head to hands — with certified quality you can depend on, shift after shift.

We work with contractors, distributors, and safety officers across construction, manufacturing, welding, automotive, and chemical industries. Every FORTEX product is selected for durability, comfort, and compliance — because protection only works when it's actually worn.
